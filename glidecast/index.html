FAQ

Questions, answered

Do my recordings get uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, auto-zoom detection, editing and exporting all happen locally in your browser. Glidecast never sees your videos.

How does auto-zoom work?

After you stop recording, Glidecast scans the video for focused on-screen activity like clicks, menus opening and typing, and adds smooth zooms at those moments. Each zoom shows on the timeline, where you can drag it, change its depth, re-aim it or delete it.

Which browsers are supported?

Desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc give the best results. Firefox works too. Safari supports recording on recent macOS versions. On phones and tablets you can import a video and polish it.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No. Open the recorder and start. Pro is unlocked with the license key we email you after checkout.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, anytime, from the billing link in your receipt email. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Absolutely. Everything you export is yours, including for client work, courses, ads and products.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.

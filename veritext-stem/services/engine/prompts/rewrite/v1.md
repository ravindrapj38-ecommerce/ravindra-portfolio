Hard rules. A sentence that breaks any of them is rejected automatically and sent back to you.
1. Tokens like ⟦M1⟧, ⟦N2⟧, ⟦C3⟧ stand for equations, numbers with units, citations, chemical or gene

3. Keep causal claims causal and correlational claims correlational. "Is associated with" must not

4. Keep every negation. Do not add or remove "not", "no", "never", "without" or similar.
5. A citation token stays attached to the same claim, right after the words it supported.
6. Do not add facts, numbers, examples or claims, and do not drop any.
7. Return exactly one rewritten sentence for each input sentence, in the same order. Do not merge or
   split sentences.

Style. Prefer plain verbs to nominalisations, concrete subjects, and varied sentence length. Avoid
filler and stock phrases ("it is worth noting that", "delve", "plays a crucial role", "furthermore"
at every turn). Active voice is fine where the register allows it. If a sentence is already good,
return it unchanged.
